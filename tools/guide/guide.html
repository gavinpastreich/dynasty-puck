Every week

Your team, and your lineup, at a glance

Lineups lock weekly, so one bad lineup costs a whole week. The app checks the lineup you've actually set in Fantrax against the best possible one, before the lock.

🔒 Fantrax lineup check (Team Hub; also on the dashboard): expected categories as set vs optimal, who to start or sit, injured or idle starters, and goalie-minimum risk, with a countdown to the lock.
Team Hub: category profile vs the league, what your team should do (FA targets, complementary trade partners, cap notes), lineup by week.

What you can do

  • See where you rank in each of the 15 categories and which ones to fix.
  • Get free-agent targets ranked by how many category wins they add to your lineup.
  • Know whether to buy or sell: contender, bubble or not contending, with buy targets or sell candidates and who'd pay most.
  • Find trade partners whose strengths match your needs (and vice versa).
  • Punt check: would ignoring a category win you more overall?
  • Plan around graduations: MNR players close to 84 career GP (42 for goalies). The lineup check warns before you promote one who's about to graduate.
  • See your cap by season with the rising cap line ($104M → $113.5M → $127.5M projected).
A 2-minute weekly routine: (1) open the Dashboard and fix anything the lineup check flags before the lock; (2) glance at Free Agents for adds that fit your categories; (3) check the Weekly Preview for your matchup's swing categories; (4) during the week, follow it on the Live Scoreboard.

Game week

Your week, day by day

Before the lock: who to add and how to beat this week's opponent. During the week: how the matchup is swinging, day by day.

📥 Pickup Planner: every free agent with games next week, added to your roster one at a time and scored against your actual opponent, with who to move to make room. "Focus on" sorts by any one category (hits, saves...).
🔭 Opponent Scouting: their likely lineup, the swing categories, and the lineup tweaks that move your odds most.
📈 Day by day (Live Scoreboard, every matchup): the chance to win after each day of the week and the categories each side led. Your own matchup's line is on the Dashboard, and every week on your Team Hub.

How to use it

  • Sunday night: Pickup Planner for adds, Opponent Scouting for the swing categories, then set your lineup.
  • During the week: the Live Scoreboard updates while games are on; "Day by day" shows how you got there.
  • The Weekly names the comeback (or upset) of the week, from the same day-by-day odds.
The day-by-day numbers come from each day's NHL stats for your locked lineup; added up they match the week's final totals exactly.

Categories

Who is strong where, and who to trade with

Fifteen categories decide every week. Category Insights shows how all 14 teams stack up in each one, so you can see what you are really short of and who has it to spare.

🧩 Category Insights: every team's rank in every category. Blue is stronger than the league, orange weaker. Switch between what has actually happened, the projection for the rest of the season, or a blend of the two.
Trade partners: all 13 teams ranked by fit, with the categories each side has to offer and the players who move them.

How to use it

  • Three views: actual results, rest-of-season projection, or a blend that leans on results more as weeks pile up. Early in the season the blend keeps a couple of odd weeks from deciding everything.
  • Your team, category by category: where you are winning, where you are losing, and who to call for each need.
  • Trade partners: a real fit runs both ways. A team ranks high only if it is strong where you are weak and wants what you have extra of.
  • Drill into one category: the players who move it most, on rosters and among free agents.
"Build trade" opens the Trade Machine with players from both sides already loaded. Also: 🎯 what wins each category and 🧬 which categories come together.

What if?

Models you can play with

Change one thing and the season is simulated again with the same random numbers, so the difference is the change.

🔮 Scenario Builder: pick winners for the next two weeks and see every team's playoff and title odds move. The picks are in the link, so you can send a scenario to the chat.
🗺️ Title Path: which one player would raise your title odds the most: the best free agents and players on other teams, each added to your lineup for the rest of the season.
🌡️ Category Outlook: next week, every team's chance to win each of the 15 categories against the average opponent. Your thinnest categories link straight to the Pickup Planner.
🧮 Model Scorecard: the odds the site gave before each lock against what happened, with a calibration chart. When the model has been too confident, it says so.
🎯 Punt Planner: give up one to three categories on purpose and see every player re-valued without them, who climbs for your build, which of your players to sell, and what it costs your weekly odds. All of these are model estimates; Rules & Methods spells out each one.

Dynasty value

Floor, ceiling and who's next

A dynasty value is a range, not one number. These show how wide it is and which young players are moving up.

Floor and ceiling (every player page; here the league's top MNR prospect): the 10th to 90th percentile of the model's outcomes, on the league's scale. Prospects get a wide bar and their chance of an NHL career; established players a narrow one. The 💎 Trade Value Chart has the same floor and ceiling as a column.
🌱 Development curve (players 25 and under): each season's scoring translated to the NHL, and the age-adjusted estimate the prospect model reads. Climbing season over season = ahead of the curve.
🚀 Breakout Watch: young skaters producing above their projection, with a bigger role, recent promotions, more ice time and a hot two weeks; plus everyone ahead of the development curve, prospects included.
⭐ Player Rater: who has produced most in our categories this season, and how far ahead of or behind his preseason projection each player is.

Finding an edge

🏷️ Market Check, punt swaps and underused scorers

Most fantasy managers think in points-league terms. Our league counts hits, blocks, PIM and takeaways too, so some players are worth more here than their reputation, and some less.

🏷️ Market Check: Fantrax ADP (the average draft position across every Fantrax NHL draft) next to our categories' rank. Green = our categories like him more than the typical drafter; red = drafters like him more. The categories behind each gap are listed. Every player page shows his ADP too.
🔁 Punt swaps (Punt Planner): give a player whose value comes from the categories you punt, get one of about the same market value who helps the ones you keep. Each opens in the Trade Machine.
⚡ Underused scorers (Ice Time): top-quarter points per 60 minutes on below-median ice time. If the minutes go up, the points usually follow.

Next summer

🔨 Auction War Room

Get ready for the 2027 UFA auction: every team's budget, the whole player pool with projected prices, and a plan for your team.

Budgets and max bids for all 14 teams, targets that fit your team and your budget, "nominate early" ideas to drain rivals' cap, your hometown-discount players, and the pool with prices and the contract length each price implies.

How prices are projected

Calibrated on this summer's auction (62 signings, $147.9M spent). Owners paid about 3-4× more per projected win for players under 27, so prices follow each player's next three seasons. They also scale with how much cap space the league will have next summer. Treat them as a guide; the 2026 auction had no true stars.

During the auction

Tap Mark sold as players go: budgets update and the remaining prices re-adjust to the money and talent left. The hometown discount (10% off for the team that held a player from the trade deadline to season's end) is shown for every player.

Prospect Radar: who is rising, who is falling, and why

Every prospect is re-scored each night from his games in every league, NHL to junior to Europe, and news about him is collected from around the web. The Radar shows what changed and the reason.

What's in it

  • One scale for everyone: your MNR players, unowned prospects, and 2026 NHL draft picks Fantrax hasn't added yet (names for the next rookie draft).

Cheat sheet

League rules, as the app uses them

Every number in the app follows these. The commissioner keeps them in one settings file, so they stay current.

Scoring15 weekly head-to-head categories. Skaters: Pt-D (points by defensemen), G, A, 2G+A, PIM, SOG, STP, Hit, Blk, Tk, Cor. Goalies: W, GAA (lower wins), SV%, SHO. Standings = total category W-L-T.
LineupsLock weekly when the Fantrax scoring period starts. 12 F / 6 D / 2 G starters + 3 bench. MNR players can be started.
Goalie minimumFewer than 2 goalie games in a week loses all four goalie categories.
Playoffs8 teams, no byes, weeks 25-27 (Mar 22 - Apr 10, 2027). Division winners are seeds 1-2, then by record. 1v8, 4v5, 3v6, 2v7.
Salary cap$104M (2026-27), $113.5M (2027-28), $127.5M projected for 2028-29 (NHL's estimate). Contracts: BID, RFA1, ELC1 ($1.5M), MNR ($0), FA.
MNR graduation84 career NHL GP (skaters) / 42 (goalies). Stays $0 the rest of that season; ELC ($1.5M, 1 or 2 years) or release in the offseason. Active-roster graduates can't be sent down.
When an ELC is upRelease, or re-sign: 2 yrs $2.5M · 3 yrs $4.0M · 4 yrs $5.5M · 5 yrs $7.0M · 6 yrs $9.0M · or one more ELC year at $1.75M (then UFA).
Post-ELC RFAUnder 27 on June 30: re-sign 2-6 years at 1.5× (2-4 yrs) or 1.75× (5-6 yrs) of the base; the base can't go down. Offer sheets allowed (offers or trade packages for his rights). 27+: UFA auction.
RFA on an auction contractBID and RFA1 players use the league formula (S = salary): 1 yr S+1 · 2 yrs 1.5S+1 · 3 yrs 1.5S+2 · 4 yrs 1.5S+3 · 5 yrs 1.75S+4 · 6 yrs 1.75S+5 ($M; 2027 numbers not final).
UFA auctionThe winning bid sets the term: $1-2.9M 1 yr · $3-4.4M 2 · $4.5-7.4M 3 · $7.5-9.9M 4 · $10-13.9M 5 · $14M+ 6.
Trade deadlineFriday, Jan 29, 2027 at 11:59 PM ET.
Hometown discountThe team that had the player from the trade deadline to season's end pays 10% less than its winning bid (the bid still sets the term).
Injured reserveA player in an IR slot in Fantrax doesn't count against this season's cap. Activating him puts his salary back on it.
Dead capDropping a BID, RFA1 or ELC contract keeps 50% of the salary for every remaining year. Dropping an FA contract is free.
League draft3 rounds. Reverse standings: worst record picks 1st, champion 14th. Picks exist through 2029; a year is added after each draft.

How it works

Where the numbers come from

Fantrax (live): rosters, contracts, salaries, lineup slots, standings, divisions, draft picks. Fantrax is always the official record.

NHL public data: stats, career games and schedules every night (84 games, real games per fantasy week); live box scores, play-by-play and shift charts during games for the scoreboard; every prospect's season line in every league.

Projections: Fantrax's 2026-27 projections, with Takeaways and Corsi projected by the app, blended with real stats once games start.

Research: injuries and line combinations from Daily Faceoff; draft boards from public rankings; prospect headlines from NHL.com and about 47 public news feeds; the league's contract sheet and trade history.

Values: WAR = expected category wins above a replacement player. Dynasty value adds up to 7 seasons with age curves, our contract rules and cap cost.

Simulations: every weekly matchup is simulated category by category (goalie minimum included) on the real schedule, thousands of times.

Projections and odds are estimates, not facts. The Rules & Methods page in the app explains every formula, and Data & Updates shows the sync status and lets you export anything to CSV.
