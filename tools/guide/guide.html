Start here

Get set up in 60 seconds

  1. Open the link on your phone or computer: gavinpastreich.github.io/dynasty-puck
  2. Pick your team (🧢 button, top right). The dashboard, Team Hub, matchups, trade ideas and cap tools tailor themselves to you. It's remembered on your device only.
  3. Add it to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone, ⋮ → Add to Home screen on Android) so it opens like an app.
  4. Turn on alerts (🔔 Alerts) if you want a lineup reminder before every lock, a calendar of locks, or the Monday email. Totally optional.
Tip: press / anywhere to search any player. Click a player's name anywhere in the app to open his card.

Always up to date

Nightly Every night (1 AM and 6 AM ET) the site refreshes NHL stats and schedules, injuries and line combos (Daily Faceoff), projections and the live scoreboard, all automatically.

Every week

Your team, and your lineup, at a glance

Lineups lock weekly, so one bad lineup costs a whole week. The app checks the lineup you've actually set in Fantrax against the best possible one, before the lock.

🔒 Fantrax lineup check (Team Hub; also on the dashboard): expected categories as set vs optimal, who to start or sit, injured or idle starters, and goalie-minimum risk, with a countdown to the lock.
Team Hub: category profile vs the league, what your team should do (FA targets, complementary trade partners, cap notes), lineup by week.

What you can do

  • See where you rank in each of the 15 categories and which ones to fix.
  • Get free-agent targets ranked by how many category wins they add to your lineup.
  • Know whether to buy or sell: contender, bubble or not contending, with buy targets or sell candidates and who'd pay most.
  • Find trade partners whose strengths match your needs (and vice versa).
  • Punt check: would ignoring a category win you more overall?
  • Plan around graduations: MNR players close to 84 career GP (42 for goalies). The lineup check warns before you promote one who's about to graduate.
  • See your cap by season with the rising cap line ($104M → $113.5M → $127.5M projected).
A 2-minute weekly routine: (1) open the Dashboard and fix anything the lineup check flags before the lock; (2) glance at Free Agents for adds that fit your categories; (3) check the Weekly Preview for your matchup's swing categories; (4) during the week, follow it on the Live Scoreboard.

Players

Every player, valued for our categories

Values are built for our 15 categories and weekly lock, not generic points. WAR = category wins above a replacement player; Dynasty value = wins over 7 seasons net of cap cost, with our contract rules applied.

Player card: 2025-26 vs 2026-27 projection by category (with percentiles), dynasty outlook by season with the contract path, career by league, NHL history, injuries and line/PP role. Once games start it shows this season's stats and a rest-of-season projection.
🛒 Free Agents: every unowned player ranked by fit for your team.
  • Players: search, sort and filter all ~1,250 players; export CSV.
  • Free Agents: best adds for your categories, waivers flagged.
  • Compare: up to four players side by side (＋ Compare on any card).
  • Leaderboards: best contracts, dynasty value, prospect pools, roster ages.
  • Tk and Cor (Fantrax projects them as 0) are projected by the app from NHL data.

Trades

Build, balance and grade trades

🔁 Trade Machine: 2-4 teams, players and draft picks. Shows value under different "lenses" (the model, the league's own trading tendencies, or your own weights for cap, patience, picks and win-now), the category impact this season, playoff odds before and after, cap after the trade, and a "balance it" suggestion. Share a link to any trade.
  • Team context: a contender and a rebuilding team value the same player differently. Every trade shows what it's worth to each team: roster fit (depth, positions, categories) × how much this season matters to them.
  • Trade Finder: "who could I get for X?" and "what would it take to get Y?", ranked by deals that help both sides.
  • League lens: fitted to our 17 real trades, so you can see how this league tends to value picks, youth and cap.
  • Trade History grades every league trade; the Trade Value Chart puts every player and pick on one scale; Draft Picks shows who owns every 2027-2029 pick.
Team context: the same deal for a contender (×3.2 win-now weight) and a rebuilding team.

Next summer

🔨 Auction War Room

Get ready for the 2027 UFA auction: every team's budget, the whole player pool with projected prices, and a plan for your team.

Budgets and max bids for all 14 teams, targets that fit your team and your budget, "nominate early" ideas to drain rivals' cap, your hometown-discount players, and the pool with prices and the contract length each price implies.

How prices are projected

Calibrated on this summer's auction (62 signings, $147.9M spent). Owners paid about 3-4× more per projected win for players under 27, so prices follow each player's next three seasons. They also scale with how much cap space the league will have next summer. Treat them as a guide; the 2026 auction had no true stars.

During the auction

Tap Mark sold as players go: budgets update and the remaining prices re-adjust to the money and talent left. The hometown discount (10% off for the team that held a player from the trade deadline to season's end) is shown for every player.

Graduation rule refresher: an MNR player graduates at 84 career NHL games (42 for goalies). He stays at $0 for the rest of that season and signs a $1.5M ELC (or is released) in the offseason. Graduating on your active roster locks him there; graduating in the minors he can stay down, but once promoted he can't go back.

Cheat sheet

League rules, as the app uses them

Every number in the app follows these. The commissioner keeps them in one settings file, so they stay current.

Scoring15 weekly head-to-head categories. Skaters: Pt-D (points by defensemen), G, A, 2G+A, PIM, SOG, STP, Hit, Blk, Tk, Cor. Goalies: W, GAA (lower wins), SV%, SHO. Standings = total category W-L-T.
LineupsLock weekly when the Fantrax scoring period starts. 12 F / 6 D / 2 G starters + 3 bench. MNR players can be started.
Goalie minimumFewer than 2 goalie games in a week loses all four goalie categories.
Playoffs8 teams, no byes, weeks 25-27 (Mar 22 - Apr 10, 2027). Division winners are seeds 1-2, then by record. 1v8, 4v5, 3v6, 2v7.
Salary cap$104M (2026-27), $113.5M (2027-28), $127.5M projected for 2028-29 (NHL's estimate). Contracts: BID, RFA1, ELC1 ($1.5M), MNR ($0), FA.
MNR graduation84 career NHL GP (skaters) / 42 (goalies). Stays $0 the rest of that season; ELC ($1.5M, 1 or 2 years) or release in the offseason. Active-roster graduates can't be sent down.
When an ELC is upRelease, or re-sign: 2 yrs $2.5M · 3 yrs $4.0M · 4 yrs $5.5M · 5 yrs $7.0M · 6 yrs $9.0M · or one more ELC year at $1.75M (then UFA).
Post-ELC RFAUnder 27 on June 30: re-sign 2-6 years at 1.5× (2-4 yrs) or 1.75× (5-6 yrs) of the base; the base can't go down. Offer sheets allowed (offers or trade packages for his rights). 27+: UFA auction.
RFA on an auction contractBID and RFA1 players use the league formula (S = salary): 1 yr S+1 · 2 yrs 1.5S+1 · 3 yrs 1.5S+2 · 4 yrs 1.5S+3 · 5 yrs 1.75S+4 · 6 yrs 1.75S+5 ($M; 2027 numbers not final).
UFA auctionThe winning bid sets the term: $1-2.9M 1 yr · $3-4.4M 2 · $4.5-7.4M 3 · $7.5-9.9M 4 · $10-13.9M 5 · $14M+ 6.
Trade deadlineFriday, Jan 29, 2027 at 11:59 PM ET.
Hometown discountThe team that had the player from the trade deadline to season's end pays 10% less than its winning bid (the bid still sets the term).
Injured reserveA player in an IR slot in Fantrax doesn't count against this season's cap. Activating him puts his salary back on it.
Dead capDropping a BID, RFA1 or ELC contract keeps 50% of the salary for every remaining year. Dropping an FA contract is free.
League draft3 rounds. Reverse standings: worst record picks 1st, champion 14th. Picks exist through 2029; a year is added after each draft.

How it works

Where the numbers come from

Fantrax (live): rosters, contracts, salaries, lineup slots, standings, divisions, draft picks. Fantrax is always the official record.

NHL public data (nightly): stats, career games, schedules (84 games, real games per fantasy week), and this season's box scores for the live scoreboard.

Projections: Fantrax's 2026-27 projections, with Takeaways and Corsi projected by the app, blended with real stats once games start.

Research: injuries and line combinations from Daily Faceoff; draft boards from public rankings; the league's contract sheet and trade history.

Values: WAR = expected category wins above a replacement player. Dynasty value adds up to 7 seasons with age curves, our contract rules and cap cost.

Simulations: every weekly matchup is simulated category by category (goalie minimum included) on the real schedule, thousands of times.

Projections and odds are estimates, not facts. The Rules & Methods page in the app explains every formula, and Data & Updates shows the sync status and lets you export anything to CSV.
